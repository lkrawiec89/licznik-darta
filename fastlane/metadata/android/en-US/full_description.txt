Dart Scoreboard is an open-source, privacy-friendly dart scorer designed for home games and tournaments.

Features:
• Multiple game modes: X01 (301, 501, 701, Double Out, Master Out), Cricket, and Round the Clock
• Practice against an AI bot with adjustable difficulty levels
• Smart checkout suggestions with preferred finishing paths
• Voice announcements and sound effects
• In-depth player and match statistics (3-dart averages, first 9, checkout %, detailed hit charts)
• 100% offline, privacy-first: no ads, no trackers
• Database backup and restore (JSON format)
